Why KFUPM

Why choose KFUPM

Degrees designed for you

From the start of your academic year, you can choose from dozens of majors or create a customised interdisciplinary undergraduate degree that is tailored to your academic and career aspirations.

Rankings that speak for themselves

KFUPM continues to climb in global recognition. According to QS World Rankings, we rose from the 200th place in 2020 to 67th today, with several disciplines ranked in the global top 100.

Life in Dhahran

KFUPM's campus is located in Dhahran, a metropolitan center and cultural landmark that welcomes people from diverse backgrounds and offers countless opportunities for entertainment, relaxation, and exploration.

Research that matters

Our philosophy is that research must be fundamental to addressing the nature of global challenges. This stance drives our work in emerging fields such as the Internet of Things (IoT), quantum computing, energy transition, smart city design, and more.

Expanding academic programs

As industries transform, so must education. KFUPM has introduced over 90 programs and specialisations, reflecting both local and global needs.
