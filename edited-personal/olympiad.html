Olympiad Admission Track

Direct undergraduate admission for students with outstanding achievements in recognized international science and mathematics Olympiads.

Application dates

  • Opens

    25 Nov 2025

  • Closes

    11 Feb 2026

  • Cycle

    12 Mar 2026

Dates can move; confirm them with the admissions office.

Admission requirements

Admission requirements
Eligibility High school graduate, or graduating in the current academic year.
Meets the applicant status requirements for the track.
Winner of a top award in one of the approved competitions.
Documents Olympiad certificate.
Recommendation letter.
Additional Never previously studied at KFUPM or its community college.

Benefits

  • Fully covered tuition fees
  • Monthly stipend
  • Free in-campus housing
  • Free textbooks
  • Discounted meals
  • Free primary medical care
  • Internship for 8 weeks

How to apply

  1. 1

    Step 1: Application

    Begin your online application and complete your track’s form.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Selecting a major

    Placement depends on your weighted average score and seat availability in your chosen major.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Nomination

    We confirm the major you are admitted to; you confirm within 24 hours and complete your profile.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Initial admission

    Granted once all required documents are uploaded within the specified time frame.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Final admission

    On meeting all criteria, you receive notification of unconditional admission.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Enrollment

    You receive a student number, gain access to e-services and become a registered student.

Frequently asked questions

More FAQs
What visas do international students need to get?

Once admitted, the University will apply for your student visa on your behalf. The process may take up to four weeks.

What support do Olympiad track students receive?

Fully covered tuition fees, a monthly stipend, free in-campus housing, free textbooks, discounted meals, free primary medical care and an internship for 8 weeks.

How easy is it to get student accommodation?

Students admitted through the Olympiad track receive free in-campus housing.

What is the application process like?

There are six steps: application, selecting a major, nomination, initial admission, final admission and enrollment. Begin your online application and complete your track’s form.
