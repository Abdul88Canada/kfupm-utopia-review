Meet our Leaders

The university’s accolades and high achievements are owed, in part, to our leaders who provide clear direction and seek to continue KFUPM’s legacy as a center of excellence.

Guided by the President and the Executive Leadership Team, leadership efforts delegate responsibilities with tactical precision, ensuring every initiative remains congruent with the university’s mission and shared goals. Their energy reflects a commitment to advancing higher education through knowledge and partnerships that evoke progress and create meaningful opportunities for faculty, researchers, and students alike.
