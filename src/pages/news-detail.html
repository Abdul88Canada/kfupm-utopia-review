Researchers at King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals have announced a breakthrough in carbon capture technology that promises to significantly reduce industrial emissions. The innovation demonstrates a novel approach to removing CO2 directly from industrial exhaust streams.

This breakthrough represents a major step forward in our ability to combat climate change while maintaining industrial productivity.

Prof. Hassan Al-Mojel, Lead Researcher
