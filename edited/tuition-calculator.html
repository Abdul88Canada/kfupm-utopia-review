Frequently Asked Questions

More FAQs
How are the discounts applied?

Discounts stack according to your eligibility. Each one is taken off the amount left after the one before it, in this order: Continuing Scholar, Lifelong Learner, Citizen, Destination KSA, then Early Bird.

For example, a Saudi continuing student applying in Cycle 1 to a 50,000 SAR program saves 60% (30,000 SAR), then 25% of the remaining 20,000 SAR (5,000 SAR), then 25% of the remaining 15,000 SAR (3,750 SAR), and pays 11,250 SAR.

Executive programs and company-sponsored applicants are charged a flat fee with no discounts. Final fees are confirmed by the Deanship of Graduate Studies on admission.

Which discounts could I be eligible for?
  • KFUPM Continuing Scholar Discount (60%) for continuing students
  • KFUPM Lifelong Learner Discount (30%) for returning students
  • Vision 2030 Citizen Discount (25%) for Saudi nationals
  • Vision 2030 Destination KSA Discount (25%) for international applicants
  • Early Bird Discount (25%) for applications in Cycle 1

The continuing and returning student types apply to KFUPM BS degree holders only. The MBA requires three years of professional experience, so it has no continuing-student route. International applicants usually apply in Cycle 1; places in later cycles are by exception, so please confirm availability with Graduate Admissions.

How is thesis-based and PhD tuition worked out?

Self-sponsored thesis and PhD students pay per semester: a registration fee plus 500 SAR for each credit hour, for 3 to 12 credit hours. The registration fee is 5,000 SAR for Saudi students and 7,500 SAR for non-Saudi students during coursework, and reduces to 2,500 SAR and 3,750 SAR at the thesis/dissertation stage.

Scholarship students pay no tuition. The scholarship also includes a stipend, on-campus housing, medical care, and other benefits. For sponsored students, the employer or sponsoring body settles the registration and credit-hour fees directly.

Can I see fees in US dollars?

Yes. Choose USD under "Show amounts in" and every figure in your estimate is converted at 3.75 SAR to the US dollar.
