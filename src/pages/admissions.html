Admission pathways

How to apply

  1. Register

    In order to register, first you will need to create your account on qabool.kfupm.edu.sa

  2. Submit documents

    Upload transcripts and test scores. Undergraduate: Qudrat + Tahsili or SAT 1350+. Graduate: GPA records and references.

  3. Initial review

    Candidacy notification with assigned major.

  4. Conditional admission

    Confirm specialisation, pending document verification.

  5. Enrolment

    University number, electronic services, orientation.

Study at KFUPM from abroad

Studying abroad is a significant step, and you should feel confident about your decision. At KFUPM, students find a supportive environment, practical programs, and global connections that prepare them for the future.

  • Visa and sponsorship

    KFUPM provides visa sponsorship after final admission.

  • English requirements

    STEP 65+, IELTS 4.5+, TOEFL iBT 45+, Duolingo 80+.

    Submit attested transcripts and degree certificates.
