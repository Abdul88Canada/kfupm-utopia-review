Tuition and Financial Support

Tuition Calculator Scholarships

KFUPM aims to provide accessible education for all. We believe exceptional talent should not be limited by financial constraints.

As such, multiple scholarship and financial support opportunities are available for students at all levels of study. For more details, you may explore available pathways and estimate costs using the tuition calculator.

Undergraduate study is tuition-free for Saudi nationals. Scholarship students pay no tuition, and the scholarship also includes a stipend, on-campus housing, medical care, and other benefits.

  • Visa: Once admitted, the University will apply for your student visa on your behalf. The process may take up to four weeks.
  • English Requirements: Minimum scores vary by program. Please check the specific program page for details.
