Each of our colleges encompasses multiple departments that both delineate the scope of their disciplines and maintain strong interdepartmental ties to enhance both teaching and research outcomes. Together, they form a visible structure that reflects academic strength and intellectual depth across diverse fields.

Our colleges collectively cover the sectors essential to Saudi Arabia’s development and the wider global economy. They are: Energy & Minerals, Chemicals & Materials, Environment & Sustainability, Tech Manufacturing, Aerospace & Defense, Digital Economy, Biotechnology, Mobility & Logistics, and FinTech.

This academic arena mirrors higher education institutions worldwide, where excellence is measured by the ability to seize emerging opportunities and address real-world challenges.

Explore our colleges and see how each one intertwines learning, research, and innovation.
