KFUPM Utopia: three versions for review

The original has three pages: Home, Leadership and the President's bio. The refined and KFUPM Branding Guidelines versions add Study, Admissions (hub, Undergraduate and Olympiad track), Apply, Colleges, Programs (list, search and detail), a working Tuition Calculator, News, a news article and a 404 page. Use the Study menu at the top of each site to move between them.
